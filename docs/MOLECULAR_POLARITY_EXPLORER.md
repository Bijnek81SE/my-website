# Molecular Polarity Explorer

Interactive lab connecting bond polarity, molecular geometry, vector cancellation, and net molecular dipoles.

## Included

- Foundation, Intermediate, and All modes
- Ten molecules from CO2 and H2O to methanol, acetone, and benzene
- Toggleable bond-dipole and net-dipole overlays
- Responsive SVG molecule diagrams
- Immediate feedback, hints, progress, and scoring
- New route: `/lab/molecular-polarity`
