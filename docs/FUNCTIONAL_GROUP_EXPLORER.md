# Functional Group Explorer

Route: `/lab/functional-groups`

The explorer provides immediate-feedback recognition practice for alcohols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, amines, amides, and nitriles.

## Interaction pattern

1. Inspect a condensed formula.
2. Choose a functional group.
3. Reveal a hint when needed.
4. On a correct answer, highlight the group and show its pattern, suffix, prefix, and example.

## Reuse

The component is exported from `components/lab` and can later be embedded in lesson pages or revision modules.
