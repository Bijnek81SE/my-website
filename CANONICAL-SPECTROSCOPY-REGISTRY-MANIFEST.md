# Canonical Spectroscopy Registry

Phase 6 of the Unified Chemistry Content & Extension Architecture.

## Purpose

Spectroscopy datasets are authoritative structured chemistry records connected to canonical molecules by stable IDs. Molecule names, formulas, atoms, and bonds are derived from the molecule registry rather than duplicated.
